About me

I’m a Product Designer working on AI-driven SaaS platforms and digital products where complexity is usually the problem. My work focuses on simplifying workflows, structuring scalable systems, and making advanced technology usable for real teams. I don’t just design interfaces — I think about how products behave, how decisions flow, and how trust is built over time.

I’ve worked across B2B SaaS, B2C mobile apps, design systems, and client projects for startups and businesses in India, California, and Australia. Most of the products I’ve worked on started with the same situation: too many manual steps, unclear systems, or experiences people struggled to understand. My role has always been to bring structure to that chaos and turn it into something people can actually use with confidence.

I collect good product experiences the way some people collect books. If I see a smooth interaction, a smart workflow, or a tiny detail that removes friction, I remember it. Same with tools and plugins: I’m always searching for better ways to think, build, and collaborate. Good design usually hides in small decisions, and I enjoy noticing the things most people overlook.

Outside of work, I’m usually around cricket, games, music, or conversations where people are figuring ideas out in real time. I enjoy brainstorming, meeting new people, and being part of discussions before the answers are obvious. I believe the best product thinking happens when curiosity, collaboration, and strong opinions come together in the same room.

I enjoy working on products that solve real operational problems. I’m especially interested in AI tools, automation systems, and platforms where design directly improves clarity, efficiency, and decision-making.

What I Specialize In

I design workflows that turn complex AI capabilities into clear, usable product experiences. My focus is on document processing, automation, and enterprise tools where users depend on accuracy, speed, and predictability.

Design Systems and Scalable UI

I build design systems that make products faster to build, easier to maintain, and consistent across features. Using tokens, reusable components, and clear interaction patterns, I create foundations that support long-term product growth.

How I Work

Problem Framing

I start by understanding the real operational problem, not just the interface request. This helps ensure the solution addresses the root issue, not just the surface symptoms.

Cross-functional Collaboration

I work closely with engineers and product stakeholders throughout the design process. This helps ensure designs are practical, technically feasible, and aligned with product goals from the beginning.

Clear Communication

I translate product decisions into clear flows, structured layouts, and documentation that teams can execute confidently. My focus is on removing ambiguity so implementation stays aligned with the intended experience.

Iterative Improvement

I continuously refine designs based on feedback, testing, and real-world constraints. Each iteration improves usability, removes friction, and strengthens the overall product experience.
