END-TO-END PRODUCT DESIGN

At Zenisth AI, I was the only designer across 6 AI tools with no template to follow. I mapped full system flows what happens before the user arrives, what happens when things break, what brings them back. I do not start with screens. I start with the structural problem.

B2B and B2C Experience

In a voice agent, one bad conversation loses a real customer. I designed not just the interface but the fallback logic, knowledge base structure, and the feedback loop that shows where the agent fails so the system prompt improves over time. On the B2C side I've designed for people who owe you nothing. No patience, no second chances. Same standard, different stakes.

Design Systems and Strategy

Built the Zenisth design system from scratch three layers of tokens primitive semantic and component-level. Every component documented with all states variants and modes. Then structured the entire system into a single design_tokens.md file so AI tools and IDEs could read it directly and generate accurate UI. A design system today has to speak to machines not just designers.

I come from an engineering background. When I look at a product, I am not just thinking about how it looks I am thinking about how it breaks, what the edge cases are, and whether the developer can actually build what I am handing off.

My process does not stop at Figma. I use AI to pressure test flows, catch edge cases I missed, and vibe code working prototypes to validate ideas before they go to development. I built this entire portfolio that way.

I work well alone and I work well in teams. At Zenisth I was the only designer. At Zidio I worked under mentorship. I know the difference between owning a problem and learning from someone who has solved it before. The best design decision I ever made was not a visual one. It was asking what happens when this goes wrong before anyone else thought to.
