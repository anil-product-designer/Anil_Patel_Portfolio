The Challenge/ 01
Unifying Disjointed Tools

Moving from manual entry to automated data extraction.

Enterprise users were struggling to manage thousands of documents across multiple disjointed tools. The existing process required manual data entry, leading to high error rates and significant time loss.

Our goal was to unify these workflows into a single, intelligent platform that could automate data extraction and provide clear oversight for human review.

The Solution/ 02
Prioritized Dashboard

Focusing human attention only where it's needed.

I designed a unified dashboard that prioritizes items requiring attention while automating the routine processing. By introducing a confidence-score system, users only needed to review low-confidence extractions, reducing manual workload by 80%.

Results/ 03
Impact & Delivery

Significant improvements in speed and accuracy.

Early testing with beta partners showed a 3x increase in processing speed and a 95% reduction in data entry errors. The new design system also reduced development time for future features.
